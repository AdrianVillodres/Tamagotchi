# Tamagotchi

This repository is about a Tamagotchi pet program based on **OOP**. You can feed the pet, play with it, and make it sleep. It has three stats: **hunger**, **energy**, and **health** (which depends on the other two). Depending on the stats, the pet will display different emotions, which may restrict certain actions.

**Link to the class diagram:** [Class Diagram](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qTdDgsMKYkxOqqYeYY2BhvC9EbVU8RVa/view?usp=sharing)

---

## Features

### Eat
You can choose between feeding the pet a **snack** or a **meal**:  
- **Snack:** Makes the pet happy and fills a small portion of its stomach. However, overfeeding snacks will cause stomach problems and make the pet sick.  
- **Meal:** Fills a considerable amount of the stomach and resets the snack accumulation count.

---

### Sleep
This feature allows the pet to **go to sleep** and restore its energy.

---

### Play
The pet’s response when playing depends on its **emotion**:  
- If happy, the pet will play normally.  
- If sad, the pet may refuse to play.  
- If angry, the pet might even bite you!  

Additionally, if the pet plays, you may **obtain a food item** (if your inventory is not full).
